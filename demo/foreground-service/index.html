Tinect

Foreground service demo

This video shows how Tinect uses a foreground service (type specialUse): the user starts a countdown, leaves the app, and the remaining time keeps updating in an ongoing notification until the timer ends. When the countdown finishes on the locked phone, the alarm screen is shown. The service starts only on the user's explicit action and stops when the countdown ends or is stopped. Recorded on an Android 16 emulator; no audio.
